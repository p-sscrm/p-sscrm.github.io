P‑SSCRM

Proactive Secure Software Supply Chain Risk Management

The uniting framework for secure software supply chain risk management of code dependencies, build infrastructure, AI models and data, and the humans who maintain them.

Teams securing a software supply chain face a crowded field of standards — SSDF, SLSA, BSIMM, NIST 800-161, and a dozen more — with overlapping vocabulary and no single through-line. P-SSCRM is that through-line: one model of Groups, Practices, and Controls, each mapped back to the sources it came from, so you can work from a single checklist and still show alignment with the standards that apply to you.

Background & citation

P-SSCRM was built by unifying the common ground across the field's government and industry initiatives and standards. It is described in a paper on arXiv and stewarded with the S3C2 research center.
