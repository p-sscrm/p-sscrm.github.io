Role assignments are a starting point, not a rulebook. They are maintained
upstream in [p-sscrm/p-sscrm](https://github.com/p-sscrm/p-sscrm){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and cover the
framework only partially — a control with no role assigned simply does not
appear here. Each control links to its full entry on
[The Framework]({{ "/framework/" | relative_url }}).
