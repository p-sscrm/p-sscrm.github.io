The National Online Informative References (OLIR) Program is a NIST effort to help subject matter experts define standardized online informative references (OLIRs) between elements of their documents, products, and services and elements of NIST documents such as the Cybersecurity Framework Version 1.1, Privacy Framework Version 1.0, NISTIR 8259A, or NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5.

Our crosswalk mapping between P-SSCRM and the NIST SSDF v1.1 has been accepted as part of the OLIR program. The mapping is the one shown on the [Standards & Frameworks]({{ "/frameworks/" | relative_url }}) page, and it is also available as an [Excel spreadsheet]({{ "/static/data/P-SSCRM_to_SSDF_CROSSWALK_20260219.xlsx" | relative_url }}).
