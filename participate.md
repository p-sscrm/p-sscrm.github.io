We are inviting organizations to take part in an **interview-based assessment**
of their software supply chain security practices, using P-SSCRM as the
instrument and following the methodology described in the
[project paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12300).

## What taking part involves

- One or more scheduled interviews with people responsible for your secure SDLC,
  build infrastructure, and release process.
- We walk through the P-SSCRM controls and record how your practices line up.
- You receive a summary of the findings for your organization.

## Who should take part

Organizations that build and ship software and want an external, structured read
on their supply chain security posture — whether or not you have used P-SSCRM
before.
